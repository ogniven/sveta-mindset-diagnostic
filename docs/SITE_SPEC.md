# 1. PURPOSE

Build a mobile-first long-form website with an interactive mini-diagnostic.

The website should help an intelligent, reflective visitor reconsider a familiar problem:

"Я знаю, чего хочу. Я примерно понимаю, что нужно делать. Почему тогда я продолжаю действовать по-старому?"

The central working hypothesis:

A person's next goal may conflict with an important way they are accustomed to defining themselves.

Old behavior may therefore persist not simply because of fear, lack of discipline, or "self-sabotage", but because it preserves something psychologically important:

- a familiar self-image;
- approval;
- independence;
- professional identity;
- modesty;
- control;
- predictability;
- belonging;
- a familiar place among other people.

The website must NOT diagnose the visitor.

The mini-diagnostic must NOT claim to determine:

- personality type;
- true self;
- Dominant Identifier;
- trauma;
- diagnosis;
- hidden "real cause".

It produces only a working hypothesis.

---

# 2. PRIMARY USER

The primary visitor is an intelligent, reflective, functional adult.

They may be:

- an expert;
- entrepreneur;
- consultant;
- manager;
- professional;
- creator;
- person in professional transition.

They often:

- understand a lot about themselves;
- dislike simplistic psychology;
- have already read books / attended therapy / reflected deeply;
- know what they want or at least know the direction;
- understand the next reasonable action;
- still repeat decisions that preserve the previous situation.

Typical areas:

- money;
- pricing;
- work;
- career;
- business;
- scale;
- leadership;
- visibility;
- blogging;
- sales;
- delegation;
- boundaries;
- relationships.

---

# 3. CORE EXPERIENCE

The user should enter thinking:

"Почему я опять не могу сделать то, что понимаю?"

And leave thinking:

"Возможно, я всё это время смотрела не туда. Мой старый выбор что-то сохраняет."

The intended psychological journey:

1. I have a practical problem.
2. I already know a lot.
3. My old behavior may have a function.
4. It may preserve an important way I define myself.
5. My next goal may conflict with that self-image.
6. I now have a more useful question.
7. I may want to investigate my specific case with Sveta.

# 7. MAIN COGNITIVE TURN

## Heading

Намного легче изменить цель, чем ответ на вопрос «кто я»

## Body

Представьте человека, который хочет высокий доход.

Сознательно — очень хочет.

Но одновременно знает про себя:

> Я не меркантильный человек.

Или:

> Я хороший специалист. Я не умею вот это всё «продавать себя».

Или:

> Я скромная. Мне неприятно выпячиваться.

Или:

> Если я действительно хороша, меня должны выбрать без активных продаж.

Теперь повышение цены становится не просто коммерческим действием.

Оно начинает угрожать тому, каким человеком человек привык себя считать.

И сопротивление становится понятнее.

Вопрос уже не только:

> Почему я боюсь поднять цену?

Гораздо интереснее:

**Что мне придётся перестать подтверждать о себе, если я её всё-таки подниму?**

# 10. DIAGNOSTIC UX

The diagnostic must:

- show one main question per screen;
- be optimized for mobile;
- show progress;
- allow Back;
- preserve previous answers;
- not reload the page between questions;
- use large tap targets;
- clearly distinguish single-choice and multi-choice;
- never request email before showing the result.

Use exactly nine logical stages Q1–Q9, with progress 1/9 through 9/9. A stage may contain one main question and a short conditional follow-up on the same screen. Follow-ups never get a separate progress number. F0/F1 may end the diagnostic early.

Transitions should be subtle.

Avoid unnecessary animations.

---

Do not expose internal IDs to the visitor.

Validation:

- goal_text, action_text, and future_self are required on the continuing main path.
- avoidance_sentence is optional.
- Every selected other option requires corresponding free text, stored separately from its ID. In Q2, goal_text also supplies the explanation for goal_area = other.
- F0/F1 do not require later answers; action_unknown must reach F1 without requiring an invented action.

Free-text display:

- Never automatically decline or grammatically interpolate arbitrary Russian free text.
- Show goal_text, action_text, future_self, and other text in separate quotations/blocks, preserving user wording.
- Example: "Вы назвали следующим шагом:" followed by a separate "[ACTION_TEXT]" block.
- Use fixed references to the quoted step/goal in result templates and center-specific questions.

# 22. INTERNAL SCORING

Use simple rule-based scoring.

No machine learning.

No external AI API in MVP.

Fixed v1.1 weights:

Primary self-description matching a center:
+3

Primary threat matching a center:
+2

Secondary threat matching a center:
+1

Self-loss matching a center:
+2

Protection behavior matching a center:
+2

Preserved value matching a center:
+2

Goal area matching a center:
maximum +1 per center

Do not adjust these weights automatically.

action_partial adds no points and does not stop the diagnostic, but prevents strong. action_unknown routes to F1. An other answer without predefined mapping receives no automatic points. Free text is not interpreted or scored automatically.

Scoring selects only the language of a working hypothesis; it is not a psychometric test.

Never display scores.

# 23. CONSISTENCY RULE

A self-description alone is NOT enough.

Example:

The user selects:

"Я хороший человек."

But then reports:

- no fear of disapproval;
- no approval-preserving behavior;
- approval is not what remains unchanged.

Do not force GOODNESS.

The algorithm must privilege cross-answer consistency.

# 24. EVIDENCE STRENGTH

Internal labels:

weak
moderate
strong
mixed

These labels describe consistency of answers, not the person.

Evidence dimensions: self, threat, loss, behavior, preserved, goal.

Count distinct supporting dimensions per center. Multiple answers inside one dimension count once: primary and secondary threats can add points but count as one threat dimension.

F0/F1 routing takes precedence. Evaluate MIXED before MODERATE because their conditions can overlap.

## strong

All conditions:

- top center score >= 8;
- support from at least 4 distinct evidence dimensions;
- top score minus second score >= 3;
- action_clarity = action_clear.

## moderate

All conditions:

- top center score >= 5;
- support from at least 3 distinct evidence dimensions;
- strong conditions are not met;
- mixed conditions are not met.

## mixed

First determine mixed candidates. A center is a mixed candidate only if all conditions hold:

- score >= 5;
- support from at least 2 distinct evidence dimensions;
- highest score across all centers minus this center's score <= 2.

Then route by the number of mixed candidates:

- Exactly 2: evidence_strength = mixed, mixed_mode = pair. Use both candidates in the approved pair flow (§32).
- 3 or more: evidence_strength = mixed, mixed_mode = complex. Use the complex flow (§32); do not artificially select two centers, use a fixed center priority, or use a random tie-break.
- Fewer than 2: mixed conditions are not met; evaluate the other evidence rules.

For complex, conflict_center and secondary_conflict_center must not imply a primary/secondary pair. Optional human formulations are display-only and follow the ranking and omission rules in §32.

## weak

All remaining cases.

Do not expose numeric scores.

## Heading

## Title

**«Старая версия себя / новый сценарий»**

## Body

Мы берём одну конкретную точку, где вы уже знаете, какого результата хотите, но продолжаете принимать решения, которые оставляют вас в прежней реальности.

На встрече мы не пытаемся разобрать всю вашу личность.

Мы смотрим:

2. Что вы фактически делаете.
3. Какую версию себя эти решения сохраняют.
